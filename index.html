FAQ

Questions, answered

I'm a complete beginner. Is Reflex right for me?

Yes. Lots of our members started from zero. The team will show you how to use the equipment and help you get started safely.

How do I join?

Send us an enquiry using the form below, message us on Instagram, or just drop in at the gym and the team will take you through the options.

Do you have offers on membership?

We run seasonal and festive offers, especially on yearly memberships. Follow @reflex_fitness_ or ask us for what's running right now.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable workout clothes, clean indoor shoes, a towel and a water bottle.
